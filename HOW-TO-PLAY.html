How to play Ski Jump

Same URL on a phone, tablet, classroom PC, or laptop.

Goal

Ski down and jump onto the matching word. Words come from the JSON pack, not a list baked into the game. Wrong jump hits a tree.

Touch / tablet

  • Tap or hold the left or right side to steer.
  • Slide up to go faster and boost into the jump.
  • Ski into a word ramp to answer.

Keyboard

  • ← → or A D — steer
  • ↑ or W — faster / boost

Audio

Baked local mp3 files only. No TTS server. No speechSynthesis.
